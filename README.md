## About

I'm a full-stack engineer with 10 years of experience designing and scaling enterprise web platforms across logistics, e-commerce, social media, and digital signage. **Laravel is where I go deepest** — it's been my primary stack since 2017, and I own applications end to end, including provisioning servers and deploying to production through **Laravel Forge**. Most of my work sits where REST API design, microservices, and microfrontend architecture meet real production constraints, so I spend as much time on query plans, caching, and release readiness as on shipping features. Right now I'm at DTDC Express Limited building Swytch, an international shipment delivery portal, as a Vue 3 microfrontend over Laravel services — and I bring AI in where it earns its place, most recently a RAG-powered chatbot answering customer queries from policy and SOP documents.
